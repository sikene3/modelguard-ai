`portfolio/`. A `supported with boundary` status means the evidence is real but narrower than a
general production claim. `Planned` means the asset or evidence does not yet exist and must not be
presented as complete. Fiverr package content is a bounded offer for future work, not completed
client-delivery evidence.

## Claims deliberately excluded

The portfolio does not claim any of the following:

- production readiness, high availability, zero downtime, or a permanent live endpoint;
- real transaction data, real-world fraud accuracy, fairness, economic savings, or business impact;
- distribution drift as proof of accuracy/performance degradation or causation;
- automatic retraining, automatic model promotion, a feature store, or real-time drift decisions;
- zero vulnerabilities, a hard USD 10 spending cap, compliance certification, or guaranteed uptime;
- exactly-once SNS delivery, row-level Firehose delivery-lateness measurement, or complete online
  label collection.

Any future asset that introduces one of those ideas must either remove it or add new, independently
reviewed evidence and a narrow boundary before publication.
